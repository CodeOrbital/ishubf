Product Catalog

Sparkling Solutions develops modular, reliable digital software built to increase operational efficiency, learning outcomes, and business productivity.

Cross-Platform Access: All products feature localized offline storage, fast load times, and responsive user interfaces available via standard desktop web browsers and WebApps for mobile devices.






Education Suite

Modular software built for institutions, educators, and individual learners.


1. Sparkling Study

Target Audience: Students & Self-Learners

Description: An interactive digital study platform offering structured flashcards, automated quiz generators, and progress analytics.

  • Offline-first local progress saving
  • Customizable subject track creation
  • Mobile and desktop progress sync

2. Sparkling Class

Target Audience: Schools, Teachers & Tutors

Description: A classroom management tool for lesson tracking, gradebook recording, and assignment distribution.

  • Automated student performance summaries
  • Lightweight portal accessible on low-bandwidth networks
  • Direct export to CSV and printable report formats

3. Sparkling Libra

Target Audience: Academic Libraries & Resource Centers

Description: Digital library cataloging software to manage e-books, physical inventory checking, and reading list archiving.

  • Fast text search indexing
  • Integrated user loan tracking system





Health Suite

Secure digital tools designed for healthcare management and patient interaction.


1. Sparkling Med

Target Audience: Medical Clinics & Independent Practitioners

Description: Lightweight health record manager and patient scheduling software engineered for privacy and simple workflow tracking.

  • Encrypted local medical history storage
  • Integrated appointment calendar and queue view
  • Cross-platform WebApp interface





Business & Productivity Suite

Tools designed to streamline small business accounting, asset management, and operations.


1. Sparkling Coin

Target Audience: Small Businesses & Freelancers

Description: Intuitive bookkeeping and transaction tracking software for managing daily expense logs, invoicing, and client payments.

  • Instant invoice generation and tracking
  • Automated weekly and monthly revenue summaries

2. Sparkling Econ

Target Audience: Enterprise Managers & Operations Teams

Description: Operational inventory and resource allocation software for monitoring stock levels, supply orders, and vendor databases.

  • Real-time stock threshold alerts
  • Multi-user role access controls
